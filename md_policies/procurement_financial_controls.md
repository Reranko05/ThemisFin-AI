---

# 1. Purpose

This manual defines financial governance requirements for procurement-related transactions and vendor settlement operations.

---

# 2. Vendor Payment Controls

All vendor payments must:
- include approved invoice references
- contain procurement authorization
- comply with regional approval limits

---

# 3. Restricted Procurement Activity

The following activities are considered high risk:
- duplicate vendor settlements
- split invoice payments
- repeated round-number transactions
- rapid sequential vendor transfers

---

# 4. Approval Controls

Procurement payments exceeding USD 300,000 require:
- dual authorization
- procurement director review
- compliance verification

---

# 5. Audit Monitoring

Automated controls review:
- payment duplication
- abnormal procurement frequency
- suspicious vendor activation
- dormant vendor reactivation

---

# 6. Escalation Requirements

Potentially suspicious procurement transactions may trigger:
- temporary settlement hold
- enhanced review
- operational escalation
- audit investigation
