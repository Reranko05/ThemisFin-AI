---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines escalation thresholds, approval hierarchy requirements, and mandatory compliance review conditions for enterprise financial operations.

---

# 2. Approval Hierarchy

| Transaction Amount | Required Approval Level |
|---|---|
| Below USD 100,000 | Operations Manager |
| USD 100,000 – 500,000 | Finance Controller |
| USD 500,000 – 2,000,000 | Regional Finance Director |
| Above USD 2,000,000 | Executive Treasury Committee |

---

# 3. Mandatory Escalation Conditions

Transactions must be escalated when:
- approval thresholds are exceeded
- dormant entities become active
- abnormal transaction velocity is detected
- foreign exchange exposure exceeds regional limits
- repeated transaction splitting patterns occur

---

# 4. Self-Approval Restrictions

Employees are prohibited from:
- approving transactions they initiated
- modifying approval workflows without authorization
- bypassing approval queues

Violations are categorized as critical compliance breaches.

---

# 5. Monitoring Triggers

Automated systems monitor:
- repeated round-number transactions
- rapid sequential transfers
- cross-region abnormal payment patterns
- duplicate transaction identifiers
- unusual operational timing activity

---

# 6. Escalation Workflow States

Transactions may transition through:
- Open
- Under Review
- Escalated
- Compliance Investigation
- Resolved

---

# 7. Audit Requirements

All escalated activities must include:
- audit timestamps
- reviewer identity
- action tracking
- remediation documentation
