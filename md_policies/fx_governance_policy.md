# Foreign Exchange Governance Policy

## Version Information

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Policy ID | FXG-2026-021 |
| Department | Treasury Governance |
| Effective Date | January 1, 2026 |

---

# 1. Purpose

This policy establishes governance standards for foreign exchange transactions executed across enterprise legal entities.

---

# 2. Approved Currency Operations

Approved settlement currencies include:
- USD
- EUR
- GBP
- INR
- JPY

Non-standard currency settlements require treasury approval.

---

# 3. FX Approval Controls

Foreign exchange transactions exceeding USD 250,000 equivalent require:
- treasury controller approval
- exchange rate validation
- settlement documentation

---

# 4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are prohibited:
- unauthorized currency conversion
- unapproved offshore settlement routing
- exchange rate manipulation
- manual FX override processing

---

# 5. Monitoring Controls

Automated monitoring systems review:
- unusual FX transaction frequency
- abnormal cross-currency exposure
- inconsistent settlement patterns
- rapid multi-currency conversions

---

# 6. Compliance Violations

Potential violations include:
- unsupported exchange activity
- unauthorized settlement processing
- missing treasury authorization
- inconsistent FX documentation

---

# 7. Operational Risk Indicators

The following patterns are considered elevated risk:
- repeated high-value FX transactions
- dormant entity FX activation
- excessive after-hours currency processing
- rapid currency conversion chains
